About Our Free Online Notepad

The DevToolBox Online Notepad is a free, browser-based text editor designed for quick note-taking, drafting content, and writing code snippets. By default, it opens in a modern, eye-friendly dark themewith an optional light mode toggle, automatic saving, and a seamless full page expansion option.

  • Default Dark Theme: High contrast, eye-comfort dark background with option for light mode

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this online notepad save my notes automatically?

Yes, every keystroke is automatically saved to your browser's persistent local storage. Even if you accidentally close the tab or reboot your computer, your notes will remain intact.

Is there a dark mode in this notepad?

Yes! The notepad opens by default in high-contrast Dark Mode to reduce eye fatigue during late-night writing or coding sessions. You can switch to Light Mode at any time with a single click.

How do I use the full-page expansion mode?

Click the 'Expand Full Page' button in the toolbar (or press Escape to exit). The editor will smoothly expand to cover your entire browser viewport with an edge-to-edge distraction-free writing environment.

Is my text private and secure?

100% private. Your notes never leave your computer. We do not use any cloud servers, databases, or third-party storage for your written text.
