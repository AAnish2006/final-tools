Frequently Asked Questions

What is the X (Twitter) character limit in 2026?

Standard free accounts on X (formerly Twitter) have a limit of 280 characters per tweet. Subscribed X Premium users can write up to 25,000 characters. Our tool checks both limits with live overflow highlighting.

How does the red overflow highlighting work?

Whenever your text exceeds the selected platform's limit, the exact characters exceeding the limit turn bright red in the editor. You can also click 'Trim to Limit' to automatically prune the excess.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time is estimated based on the standard average adult reading speed of 200 words per minute (wpm).

What is keyword density in this word counter?

Keyword density indicates the percentage frequency of key words in your text, helping content writers and SEO professionals prevent keyword stuffing while optimizing for search engines.
