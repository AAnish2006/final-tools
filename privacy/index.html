Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 6, 2026

Introduction

At DevToolBox ("we," "our," or "us"), your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website and use our free online tools.

Information We Collect

Data You Provide to Our Tools

Text entered into the Online Notepad, Word Counter, and JSON Formatter is processed by the tool code in your browser. The notepad stores text in your browser local storage. The tool code does not send that content to our server. The page itself loads Google Analytics and Google advertising resources, which may process visit, device, cookie, and network information. Avoid entering sensitive information into any website if you do not accept those third-party requests.

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Referring URL
  • Network and device information, which may include an IP address when a browser requests a page or third-party resource

Google Analytics and Google advertising services are loaded on the site and may collect information as described in their policies. The exact information and retention depend on provider settings and your browser or account choices.

How We Use Your Information

We use the automatically collected information to:

  • Improve our website and tools
  • Understand how users interact with our services
  • Diagnose technical issues
  • Analyze usage trends

Third-Party Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our website. These services may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies for essential functionality and analytics. Third-party ad services may also set cookies. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect the limited information we collect. Tool inputs are handled in the browser by the current tool implementations. Local processing does not make a device or browser immune to risks, and the site still makes requests to analytics and advertising providers.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact Us

This site does not currently publish a public email address or contact form. The About page identifies the site maintainer.
