About DevToolBox

DevToolBox is a free, open platform that provides essential developer tools right in your browser. Our mission is to make everyday development tasks faster, simpler, and more accessible.

Our Mission

We believe that essential developer tools should be free, fast, and private. Too many online tools are bloated with ads, require signups, or send your data to remote servers. DevToolBox is different — every tool runs entirely in your browser, ensuring your data stays on your device.

What We Offer

⏱️ Online Stopwatch & Timer

Millisecond-precision stopwatch with split lap tracking, countdown timer presets, and fullscreen display.

📝 Online Notepad

A distraction-free text editor with default dark mode, auto-save, and full-page expansion.

🔢 Word Counter

Real-time word, character, and sentence analytics with social media character limit validation.

{ } JSON Formatter

Format, validate, beautify, and minify JSON data with instant syntax error detection.

🖼️ Image Resizer

Resize JPG, PNG, and WEBP images locally using HTML5 Canvas with custom dimensions and social presets.

🔑 Password Generator

Cryptographically secure password and passphrase generator with entropy score and crack-time metrics.

📦 Base64 & URL Encoder

Encode and decode UTF-8 text, URL query strings, and convert image and document files into Data URIs.

📝 Markdown Previewer

Live side-by-side Markdown to HTML editor and previewer with formatting toolbar and instant export.

🆔 UUID / GUID Generator

Generate RFC 4122 v4 UUID identifiers in bulk with custom casing, and convert Unix epoch timestamps.

🎨 Color Converter & Contrast

Convert HEX, RGB, and HSL colors, inspect tints and shades, and audit WCAG 2.1 AA/AAA contrast ratios.

Our Values

  • Privacy First: All processing happens locally in your browser. We never collect or store your data.
  • Always Free: Our core tools will always be free to use with no signup required.
  • Quality Over Quantity: We focus on building fewer, better tools rather than a hundred mediocre ones.
  • Accessibility: Our tools are designed to work on any device, any browser, for everyone.

Built for Developers, by Developers

DevToolBox is built by a team of experienced developers who understand the daily frustrations of searching for reliable online tools. We use these tools ourselves every day, and we're committed to keeping them fast, reliable, and free.
